0. [snoopdoop](https://snoopdoop.app/i/)
   - by [Tarek Galal](https://github.com/tgalal)
   - Let's 2 people find people they know in common in a funny way. Uses Crypto to find Set-Intersection privately.
1. [Versatiles Map Animator](https://github.com/versatiles-org/versatiles-map-animator)
   - by [Michael Kreil](https://github.com/MichaelKreil)
   - Map animations to "fly" from one view of one location to the other location view.
2. [PCD 2026](https://abd.ooo/pcd-2026)
   - [by Abdo](https://abd.ooo)
   - Creative colaborative processing demo/game.
3. AI Discussion
   - by Kevin
   - Ramblings about AI, agents and translations.
4. Self made Terminal
   - A Terminal with many shortcuts and integrated special features.
5. [Audited Package-Manager](https://github.com/TriplEight/AuPM)
   - by [Denis](https://abd.ooo)
   - AuPM is an npm-compatible registry that tells you, whether a human auditor has read that tarball. Possibly pay for a review by a human.
6. [Linux driver for DJ controller](https://codeberg.org/eloi_mg/newmark-ns7ii)
   - by [eloi](https://codeberg.org/eloi_mg)
   - open-source Linux driver and Mixxx mapping for the Numark NS7 II DJ controller.
7. c-base installation **Hack of the Month**
   - by [excds](https://github.com/excds)
   - A installation where you can fly the c-base through the galaxy.
